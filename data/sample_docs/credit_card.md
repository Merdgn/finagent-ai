# FinAgent Demo - Credit Card

Document Type: Product Information
Product: Demo Credit Card

## Overview

The Demo Credit Card is a fictional banking
product used for conversational AI testing.

## Application

Applicants may submit a credit card request
through a digital application process.

Approval depends on eligibility checks
and the issuing institution's policies.

## Credit Limit

Credit limits are determined following
risk assessment and eligibility evaluation.

## Payment

Customers should review their payment
due dates and account statements.

## Security

Customers should never share PINs, passwords,
or one-time verification codes with a chatbot.

## Lost Card

A lost card should be reported immediately
through the issuing bank's official channels.

## Disclaimer

This document does not describe an actual
banking product or provide financial advice.
